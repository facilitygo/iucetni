The app is free. So is the AI that writes your invoices.

What it costs

CZK 0 from us. The Free plan has no limit on the number of invoices. Connecting over MCP is free in every plan and working with your data over MCP uses nothing up. You pay for the AI in Claude, ChatGPT or Grok through your own Anthropic, OpenAI or xAI subscription.

  • The app runs on one device of your choice. Claude, ChatGPT or Grok doesn't count, so you can use them in a browser on your computer, for example.
  • Whether custom connectors (MCP) are available depends on your plan with the AI provider.
  • If you want the AI to email invoices, connect a mailbox at namailu.cz as well. Prices are listed there.

What you do in the app and what in the conversation

In the appIn Claude, ChatGPT or Grok
Set up your company, bank account and numbering, and adjust the invoice design.“Invoice company ID 27082440 CZK 18,000 for a website.”
Photograph a receipt right at the till.“How much have I spent on materials this year?”
See an overview of invoices, payments and expenses.“Who hasn't paid yet? Send the overdue ones a reminder.”
Issue an invoice on the spot at a customer's, with a QR payment.“Same invoice as last month, just for November.”
Print a document or export data for POHODA, Money or ABRA.“Prepare my tax return figures for 2026.”

Whatever the AI prepares in a conversation shows up in the app right away, and vice versa. Nothing to retype or import.

In the morning you photograph a receipt at your supplier in the app on your phone. In the afternoon you open ChatGPT or Grok on your computer and type:

Getting started

  1. Install the app on one device. iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android, Windows or Linux. Request access on the Test access page.
  2. Create your account and company. Enter your company ID, bank account and invoice numbering. Company details are filled in from ARES.
  3. Connect iÚčetní to your AI. In Claude, ChatGPT or Grok, add a custom connector (MCP) with this URL:
    https://mcp.iucetni.com/mcp/
    Sign in with your iÚčetní account and choose which companies the AI can access. Step-by-step instructions are on the MCP server page.
  4. Work the way that suits you. Quick things in the app, writing and overviews in the conversation. To do everything in the conversation, see Invoicing with AI.

What's in the Free plan

  • Invoices, advance invoices and corrective documents with QR payment and ISDOC in the PDF.
  • Customers from ARES and a price list.
  • Receipts from photos and expenses.
  • Tax records, overviews and reports.
  • MCP server for Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and other AIs, with no limit, not counted as a device.
  • Data stored safely in the cloud on servers in the EU.
  • A trial AI assistant inside the app with a limited number of requests. You can buy AI credit even without Pro.

When Pro helps

The Free plan is for one user on one device. Pro is worth it once you want the app on several devices with the same data, to invite a colleague or your accountant, to have the AI assistant included in the app, or to keep data locally on your device too. It costs CZK 99 a month or CZK 749 a year. See pricing.

You stay in control

  • The AI prepares drafts. Invoices from the AI are marked “Draft · AI” and get a number only when issued. The AI records a payment only after you confirm and cannot change locked documents.
  • Revoke access any time in the app under Settings → AI keys.
  • Where data goes. We store it on servers in the EU. Only what the AI requests in the conversation is passed to the AI provider you connect. See the Privacy Policy.
