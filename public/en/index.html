Features

Everything for invoicing, nothing extra

Issue a document, send it and always know who owes you. The app takes care of the legal requirements of Czech invoices.

Invoices, advances, credit notes

Invoices, advance invoices and corrective tax documents. A live PDF preview while you type and numbering by pattern, e.g. 20260001.

QR payments and ISDOC

Every invoice carries a QR payment code. The ISDOC 6.0.2 e-invoice is embedded right in the PDF, so your customer's accounting software can import it.

Customers from ARES

Enter a company ID and the app fills in the name, VAT ID and address from the Czech business registry. Set custom due dates, short names and internal notes.

Price list

Saved items with internal codes go into an invoice with one tap. Items for the selected customer are shown first.

VAT and foreign currencies

21, 12 and 0% rates, reverse charge, EU supplies, exports and exempt supplies. Foreign currency invoices get the Czech National Bank rate and a VAT summary in CZK automatically.

Overviews and reports

Invoiced, paid, unpaid and overdue at a glance, plus monthly revenue charts, per-customer statistics and reports in PDF and CSV.

Receipts and supplier invoices

Snap a receipt. The details fill themselves.

No more retyping expenses. Take a photo of a receipt or drop in a supplier's PDF invoice and the app reads it for you.

  • Photo, scan or PDF. The scanner crops and straightens the document; multi-page documents are saved as one PDF.
  • Automatic fill-in. Supplier, company ID, document number, date, amount and VAT. PDFs from accounting software are read straight from their text layer, which is even more accurate.
  • The image stays with the expense. The original document is always one tap away.
  • ISDOC straight into expenses. Import a supplier's e-invoice with all its details.
Working with your accountant

Your accountant gets the data in the software they use

Export issued invoices for Czech accounting software, and move over from other invoicing tools by import.

  • Export issued invoices for POHODA, Money S3 and ABRA Flexi. Files validate against the vendors' official XSD schemas.
  • Import from POHODA XML. Bring over documents from Pohoda, Fakturoid or iDoklad, which all export this format.
  • ISDOC for customers and for importing received invoices into expenses.
  • Income and expense ledger and reports in PDF and CSV.

Security and privacy

Your numbers are safe

Data is stored on servers in the EU. No ads, no third-party tracking. Your data is yours and you can export it at any time.

Face ID and Keychain

Credentials live in the Keychain. Lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID.

Works offline

See your data without a signal; changes are sent once you're back online. A per-device number series prevents clashing invoice numbers.

No overwrites

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who is iÚčetní for?

Sole traders and small businesses in the Czech Republic that issue invoices and keep tax records. It works for VAT payers, non-payers and identified persons. The app itself is in Czech; this English site mainly serves partners and developers connecting over MCP.

How much does it cost?

On one device, invoicing and tax records with data in the cloud are free, including the MCP server for Claude and ChatGPT. Pro adds unlimited devices, multiple users per company, the in-app AI assistant and on-device data storage; it costs CZK 99 a month or CZK 749 a year, with a 7-day free trial. Pro includes AI with the Claude Haiku model for 10 invoices and 20 receipts every 30 days; more is available as AI credit, which you can also buy in the Free plan. AI with your own key and over MCP is paid to the provider. A limited trial AI in the Free plan is provided by us.

Can I use iÚčetní on several devices or with a colleague?

Yes, with Pro. The Free plan is for one user on one device. To switch to another device, sign out on the original one. Claude or ChatGPT connected over MCP doesn't count towards the limit. With Pro you get iPhone, iPad and Mac with the same data, and you can invite up to 5 more people to your company, such as a colleague or your accountant, with edit or read-only access. The company owner's subscription covers them too, and their AI use is drawn from the owner's allowance.

Where is my data stored?

On servers in the European Union. With Pro you can also keep data locally on your device.

How do I connect iÚčetní to Claude or ChatGPT?

Add a custom connector with the address https://mcp.iucetni.com/mcp/ and sign in with your iÚčetní account. Step-by-step instructions are on the MCP server page.

How is AI counted and how do I work with it?

AI is counted per item: each saved invoice uses one invoice and each created or read receipt uses one receipt. A conversation without a saved invoice or receipt uses no allowance. The app shows how many are left. The assistant works fastest when you say everything at once: “Novák job: 6 h labour, 38 km and the material from today's receipt, usual markup”. Keep items in your price list with codes so the AI finds them right away. For general chatting, use your own Claude or ChatGPT app over MCP. The iÚčetní assistant is meant for working with invoices and receipts.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS / iPadOS 26 and Mac with macOS 26 or later. It is a single App Store app for all of them.

Can I move over from Pohoda, Fakturoid or iDoklad?

Yes. The Mac app imports documents from POHODA XML, which Pohoda, Fakturoid and iDoklad all export. Issued invoices can be exported for POHODA, Money S3 or ABRA Flexi.

How do I delete my account?

In the app under Settings → Account. Export your data first, because accounting documents must be archived. See Support for details.
