Why work in Claude or ChatGPT

  • One window for everything. You already write offers, emails and texts in Claude or ChatGPT. Issue the invoice in the same conversation without switching apps.
  • Just say what you need. “Invoice the Nováks for 6 hours of consulting like last time.” The AI finds the customer, price list and earlier invoices on its own.
  • Works wherever Claude or ChatGPT does. On the web, in the Mac and Windows apps and on your phone, including Android or someone else's computer.
  • The AI costs you nothing with us. The conversation runs in your Claude or ChatGPT subscription. You don't need AI credit or Pro for everyday work.

Getting started

  1. Create your iÚčetní account and company. You do this once in the app for iPhone, iPad or Mac: enter your company ID, bank account and invoice numbering. After that you don't need to open the app.
  2. Add the connector. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector; in ChatGPT, add a custom MCP connector (app). Use this URL:
    https://mcp.iucetni.com/mcp/
  3. Sign in and approve access. The iÚčetní sign-in page opens. Sign in with your account and choose which companies the AI can access.
  4. Start typing. For example, “Who hasn't paid me yet?” Step-by-step instructions for each app are on the MCP server page.

What you type and what the AI does

You typeThe AI
“Invoice company ID 27082440 for a website, CZK 18,000, due in 14 days.”Looks the company up in ARES, adds the customer and prepares a draft invoice.
“Same invoice as last month for Kavárna U Mostu, just for November.”Finds the original invoice, copies the items and changes the period.
“Here's a photo of a receipt from Bauhaus, add it to expenses.”Reads the receipt and records an expense with the amount, VAT and supplier. Free when Claude or ChatGPT reads it itself.
“Who hasn't paid yet and how much is overdue?”Lists unpaid invoices sorted by due date.
“Invoice 2026-0042 was paid today.”Records the payment once you confirm.
“How much did I earn this year and what were my expenses?”Totals income and expenses for the year and prepares an overview for your tax return.
“Send me the PDF of the Novák invoice.”Prepares the PDF of the issued invoice, optionally with ISDOC, for sending by email.

If your Claude or agent also has a mailbox connected, for example at namailu.cz, it can read an order from an email, prepare the invoice and send it to the customer. See the example in use cases.

What it costs

Connecting over MCP is free in every plan, including Free. You pay for the AI in Claude or ChatGPT through your own Anthropic or OpenAI subscription. Invoices, expenses, overviews and PDFs over MCP use none of your AI allowance, so you don't need AI credit or the AI allowance in Pro.

  • The exception is receipt reading by our server (the read_receipt tool). With Pro it counts as one receipt; in the Free plan it runs in a limited trial mode. When Claude or ChatGPT reads the receipt itself and only records the expense, nothing is counted.
  • A connected AI app doesn't count as a device. The Free plan is enough: one device with the app, plus Claude or ChatGPT.
  • Pro is worth it once you want the app on more devices, to invite a colleague or accountant, or to use the AI assistant inside the app. See pricing.
  • Whether custom connectors are available depends on your Claude or ChatGPT plan.

You stay in control

  • The AI prepares drafts. Invoices from the AI are marked “Draft · AI” and get a number only when issued.
  • You confirm the important steps. The AI records a payment only after you confirm. It cannot change or unlock locked documents, and cancelling a payment is left to you.
  • Revoke access any time. In the app under Settings → AI keys you see every connection and when it was last used. You can limit access to selected companies.
  • Where data goes. We store your data on servers in the EU. The conversation takes place with the provider of the app you connect, Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT), and only what the AI requests in the conversation is passed to it. See the Privacy Policy.

When the app helps

Without the app you can handle everyday invoicing, expenses and overviews. The iÚčetní app is useful for creating your account and company, adjusting invoice design and numbering, quickly photographing receipts, printing, exporting for your accountant (POHODA, Money, ABRA) and emailing invoices straight from iÚčetní. Both work with the same data, so whatever the AI prepares in a conversation shows up in the app right away.
