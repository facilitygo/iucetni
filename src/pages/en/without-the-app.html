Tell Claude or ChatGPT what you need. Over MCP, the AI issues the invoice, emails it to your customer, keeps an eye on payment, sends records to your accountant and at year end prepares the figures for your tax return. Nothing to learn, nothing to retype, nothing to click. Connecting is free in every plan. Besides Claude and ChatGPT, it works with Gemini, Grok, Muse, Mistral and other AI apps and models that support MCP.

iÚčetní issues the invoice over MCP; the email goes out from a mailbox at namailu.cz, which the AI also has connected over MCP. You just give the instruction and confirm.

What the AI does

  • Invoices. Issues invoices, advance invoices and credit notes. Looks customers up in the ARES registry by company ID and takes items from your price list or a previous invoice.
  • Email. Sends the invoice with its PDF to the customer from your namailu.cz mailbox. It can also read an order that arrives there and invoice it.
  • Payments. Tells you who hasn't paid and who is overdue, records payments and can send a polite reminder.
  • Expenses. Send a photo of a receipt into the conversation and the AI records the expense with amount, VAT and supplier.
  • Cash receipts. Issues a receipt for a cash or card sale and totals the day's takings.
  • Records for your accountant. Emails your accountant PDFs of issued invoices and an income and expense overview for the month, quarter or year.
  • Tax return figures. Prepares your book of income and expenses, compares actual expenses with the 80, 60, 40 or 30 % flat rate and calculates the partial tax base under Section 7. You or your accountant then file the return.

Getting started

  1. Create your iÚčetní account and company. You do this once in the app for iPhone, iPad or Mac: enter your company ID, bank account and invoice numbering. After that you don't need to open the app.
  2. Connect iÚčetní to Claude or ChatGPT. In Claude, open Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector; in ChatGPT, add a custom MCP connector (app). Use this URL:
    https://mcp.iucetni.com/mcp/
    Then sign in with your iÚčetní account and choose which companies the AI can access. Finally, in the connector's permissions allow both the read and the write tools, otherwise the AI can't issue invoices (how).
  3. Connect email. Create a mailbox for your AI at namailu.cz and connect it the same way. The AI sends invoices to customers and records to your accountant from it.
  4. Start typing. For example, “Who hasn't paid me yet?” Step-by-step instructions for each app are on the MCP server page.

What it costs

Connecting iÚčetní over MCP is free in every plan, including Free. You pay for the AI in Claude or ChatGPT through your own Anthropic or OpenAI subscription. Working with your data over MCP uses none of your AI allowance, so you don't need AI credit or Pro for it.

  • A connected AI app doesn't count as a device, so the Free plan is enough. See The free app, with AI on top.
  • Mailbox prices are on namailu.cz.
  • Pro is worth it once you want the app on more devices, to invite a colleague or accountant, or to use the AI assistant inside the app. See pricing.
  • Whether custom connectors are available depends on your Claude or ChatGPT plan.

You stay in control

  • The AI prepares drafts. Invoices from the AI are marked “Draft · AI” and get a number only when issued.
  • You confirm the important steps. The AI records a payment only after you confirm. It cannot change or unlock locked documents, and cancelling a payment is left to you.
  • Revoke access any time. In the app under Settings → AI keys you see every connection and when it was last used. You can limit access to selected companies.
  • Where data goes. We store your data on servers in the EU. The conversation takes place with the provider of the app you connect, Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT), and only what the AI requests in the conversation is passed to it. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Not tax advice. Overviews are supporting material. You or your accountant are responsible for the tax return.

When the app helps

You need the app once to create your account and company. After that it helps when you want to adjust invoice design and numbering, quickly photograph receipts, print, or export data for accounting software (POHODA, Money, ABRA). The app and the AI work with the same data, so whatever the AI prepares in a conversation shows up in the app right away.
