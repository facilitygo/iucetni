What's new in iÚčetní

An overview of what's new in the app and in connecting to AI. You can try the app through test access.

November 2026

Rebilling expenses

  • Pass materials or services bought for a customer on straight from the expense: choose the customer and whether to bill the whole expense or selected items.
  • Set the price with or without VAT, from the purchase price with a markup (including rounding), or from your price list. You can save the item to the price list right away.
  • Invoice creates a new invoice or adds the items to a draft. Rebilled expenses are labelled in the list, so nothing is billed twice.

Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app

  • In Account, add a second step to your password: a code from an authenticator app (TOTP) such as Apple Passwords, Google Authenticator or 1Password. Set it up by scanning a QR code.
  • You get backup codes in case you lose your phone, and password reset works with the code too.
  • After several failed attempts, sign-in pauses for a while to protect your account.

Document list

  • Search by date (day, month or range) and by amount, such as “1,210 CZK”, or by an amount range.
  • Sort by customer short name, amount and payment date. The notes column can be widened to show the internal note.

Advances and final invoices

  • Deduct several advances from one final invoice, both taxed and untaxed. VAT is deducted per rate and, in a foreign currency, at each advance's exchange rate.
  • Everything carries over correctly to the PDF, ISDOC and POHODA export and import.

Language setting

  • In Settings, choose System, Čeština or English. Documents and PDFs stay in Czech.

AI assistant

  • Before writing anything, it shows a clear card with the details in plain language, names instead of codes and items as a list. Once you confirm, the card collapses.
  • Read a saved expense again with a more accurate model. You see the price first and the details are replaced only when you save. You can report a wrong reading.

Cash register and receipts

  • Issue a cash receipt by tapping items from your price list. A receipt in progress can be parked and finished later.
  • For each price list item, choose whether it's offered at the register, on invoices or both.
  • Returns: refund a whole receipt or selected items with a corrective document. The app makes sure no more is returned than was sold.
  • Convert a receipt into an invoice afterwards, for example when the customer wants a company invoice.
  • The register handles CZK, EUR and USD and rounds cash according to the currency and payment method.
  • Receipts have their own list and number series, and each register can have its own name.
  • Claude or ChatGPT can issue and list receipts over MCP too.

Reading documents with AI

  • From a photo of a receipt or invoice, the AI lists the line items and splits the document by VAT rate.
  • It now also reads the supplier's VAT ID and country, the document type and the VAT breakdown, and matches the document against delivery notes.
  • It warns you about an expense that's already recorded (a possible duplicate) and about a suspicious amount.

Expenses and tax records

  • Expenses have line items, and you set tax deductibility for each.
  • Expenses with VAT details: document type, supplier VAT ID, date of taxable supply, reverse charge and a VAT summary with checked totals.
  • Move an expense to another company.
  • For each company, choose actual expenses, flat-rate expenses or the flat-rate tax.

Working with your accountant

  • Export expenses to accounting software. Each document shows whether it has been exported, so nothing is sent twice.
  • Import documents from POHODA XML and ISDOC.
  • More precise ISDOC and PDF: line VAT matches the VAT summary, advance payments and their deduction, credit notes linked to the original document.
  • A document without items can no longer be issued by mistake.

AI assistant in the app

  • Select documents in a list and the assistant uses them as context for your question, such as “send a reminder for these invoices”.

English

  • The app is available in Czech and English and follows your device's language. Documents and PDFs stay in Czech as Czech law requires.

Invoicing with AI over MCP

  • Besides Claude and ChatGPT, you can now connect iÚčetní to Grok and Mistral.
  • Connecting over MCP is free in every plan and doesn't count as a device. See Invoicing with AI and The free app, with AI on top.
